# Trust Center Public MVP Content Spec

Status: HU/EN content specification only, no public route launch.
Last updated: 2026-06-13

Planned future routes:

- HU: `/hu/megfelelosegi-kozpont`
- EN: `/en/trust-center`

This spec gives exact draft content for a later route implementation. It does
not create routes, sitemap entries, hreflang clusters, downloadable proof
documents or structured-data credential expansion.

## Content Principles

- Keep copy factual, procurement-friendly and proof-safe.
- Do not turn legal facts into guarantees.
- Do not publish internal evidence.
- Link to existing public documents only.
- Keep D&B AA, liability insurance and AutoWallis Pest Trust Center inclusion
  behind the open decisions listed below.
