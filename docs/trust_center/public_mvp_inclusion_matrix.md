# Trust Center Public MVP Inclusion Matrix

Status: content specification source, no public route launch.
Last updated: 2026-06-13

Decision values:

- `include_mvp`: can appear in the first HU/EN Trust Center MVP.
- `include_summary_only`: can appear only as scoped summary/link text; no raw
  proof document or broad claim.
- `include_legal_detail_only`: can appear only as regulated-activity legal
  information with legal-page links; no service route, CTA, contact-dropdown
  option or marketing proof card.
- `exclude_internal_only`: internal/legal-only item; do not render in public
  MVP.
- `exclude_pending_review`: do not render until owner/legal/proof review clears
  the item.
- `later_phase`: useful later, not in the public MVP.
